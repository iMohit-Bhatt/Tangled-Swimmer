Tangled Swimmers Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026

Tangled Swimmers is a puzzle game made by MohitInTheCloud. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game stores on your phone

The game saves the following on your device only. It is never sent to us, and we have no server that receives it.

  • The name you type on the welcome screen, if you choose to give one.
  • The swimmer colour you pick.
  • Your progress: the levels you have cleared, your stars, your hints and your daily streak.
  • Your sound setting.

Deleting the game from your phone deletes all of this.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google's advertising software in the game may collect and use:

  • Your device's advertising ID.
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location.
  • Information about your device, such as its model and operating system.
  • How you interact with ads in the game, and diagnostic information.

This information goes to Google, not to us. Google may use it to show you personalised ads, depending on your choices and where you live. You can read how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy button on the levels screen.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

Sharing your daily result

If you tap "Share result", the game hands a picture of your result to the app you choose, such as a messaging app. The game does not send it anywhere itself.

Children

Tangled Swimmers is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
